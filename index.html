About the studio

Made by hand, on purpose

Picasoo & Threads is a two-person studio proving that "handmade" can still mean something in a world of print-on-demand.

How we started

A sketchbook, a sewing box, and one stubborn idea

Picasoo & Threads began in 2018 as two disciplines sharing one studio table: painting and embroidery. Neither felt complete alone — paint couldn't hold texture the way thread could, and thread alone couldn't hold a likeness. So we combined them.

Today every commission still passes through both hands before it ships: one for the underpainting, one for the stitching that gives it depth you can feel with your fingertips.

From sketch to shipment

How a piece gets made

Every commission follows the same five-stage path, start to finish, usually four to six weeks.

  1. Stage 1

    Conversation

    A call or written brief: your photos, your story, the mood you want on the wall.

  2. Stage 2

    Sketch & plan

    A graphite sketch and a materials plan — canvas size, thread palette, timeline, price.

  3. Stage 3

    Underpainting

    Meera blocks in color and light in oil or gouache; you'll see a progress photo here.

  4. Stage 4

    Threadwork

    Iven hand-dyes thread to match, then stitches texture, detail, and depth into the piece.

  5. Stage 5

    Finish & ship

    Varnish or steam-finish, frame if requested, and ship in a padded, trackable crate.

Common questions

Before you commission

How long does a commission take?

Most pieces take four to six weeks from approved sketch to shipment, depending on size and stitch density.

Can I request revisions to the sketch?

Yes — sketch review is included, and we won't move to painting until you approve it.

Do you ship internationally?

We do. International pieces ship in padded crates with tracked, insured freight.

What sizes are available?

From 12"×12" studies up to 40"×60" statement pieces; talk to us about anything larger.
